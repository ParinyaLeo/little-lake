How to Play

🎣 Cast
Pick an island on the Town Map and press CAST. Then wait for a bite.
❗ Bite
When a big "!" pops up over the bobber, press HOOK! within 1 second or the fish swims away.
🟩 Reel
A marker slides across the meter. Press REEL while it is inside the green zone. 3 hits catch the fish. Each miss costs a ❤ heart; miss with no hearts left and the fish escapes. A better rod gives more hearts.
🎒 Bag
KEEP puts your catch in the bag; sell it there for coins. If the bag is full you can sell a fish, open the bag or release the new one. You can't cast while the bag is full.
🪱 Bait
While USE BAIT is ON (button on the fishing screen), each cast uses one bait and Rare or better fish bite more often. Turn it OFF to save bait.
🛒 Shop
Spend coins on rod upgrades (one more miss allowed), bait and a bigger bag. Each item shows what it will do before you buy.
⭐ Island Mastery
Find all 10 fish on an island to unlock its reward: Golden Hook, Steady Hands, Lucky Lure or Master's Charm. Rewards work automatically. Master all four islands to discover a secret lake.
🧰 Tackle Box
Open it from Home to see your rod, bait, bag and every island reward, with what each one does.
💾 Saving
Progress saves automatically in this browser. The downloaded game file and the website link keep separate saves.
