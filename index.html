How to Play

🎣 Cast
Pick an island on the Town Map and press CAST. Then wait for a bite.
❗ Bite
When a big "!" pops up over the bobber, press HOOK! within 1 second or the fish swims away.
🟩 Reel
A marker slides across the meter. Press REEL while it is inside the green zone. 3 hits catch the fish. Each miss costs a ❤ heart; miss with no hearts left and the fish escapes.
🎣 Rods
Buy rods once in the Shop. Equip one in the Tackle Box, or tap ROD on the fishing screen to pick from a list: Bamboo (free, 1 miss), Sturdy (2 misses), Precision (35% wider green zone) or Lucky (better odds, but a 10% faster marker).
🎒 Bag
KEEP puts your catch in the bag; sell it there for coins. If the bag is full you can sell a fish, open the bag or release the new one. You can't cast while the bag is full.
🪱 Bait
Choose Worm, Shrimp, Golden Lure or no bait in the Tackle Box, or tap BAIT on the fishing screen to see every bait with its odds and pick one. Each cast uses one of the chosen bait for better odds of rarer fish.
🛒 Shop
Spend coins on rods, bait and a bigger bag. Each item shows its exact effect and the odds before you buy.
⭐ Island Mastery
Find all 10 fish on an island to unlock its reward: Golden Hook, Steady Hands, Lucky Lure or Master's Charm. Rewards work automatically. Master all four islands to discover a secret lake.
🧰 Tackle Box
Open it from Home to equip a rod, choose bait, and see your bag and every island reward. Rewards add to your rod (e.g. Master's Charm gives +1 miss).
💾 Saving
Progress saves automatically in this browser. The downloaded game file and the website link keep separate saves.
