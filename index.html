How to Play

🎣 Cast
Pick an island on the Town Map and press CAST. Then wait for a bite.
❗ Bite
When a big "!" pops up over the bobber, press HOOK! within 1 second or the fish swims away.
🟩 Reel
A marker slides across the meter. Press REEL while it is inside the green zone. 3 hits catch the fish. Miss too often and it escapes. A better rod allows more misses.
🎒 Bag
Keep your catch in the bag and sell it for coins. You can't cast while the bag is full.
🛒 Shop
Spend coins on rod upgrades, bait (better odds of rare fish) and a bigger bag.
⭐ Island Mastery
Find all 10 fish on an island to unlock its reward. Master all four islands to discover a secret lake.
